# Questions for the advocate

Please answer in your own words. Short answers are fine. "Don't know" is a good answer.

## Your filing today
1. Which courts do you file in most (High Court or district court, which state)? Roughly how many e-filings a month?
2. Which case types are most common for you? Which would you want help with first?
3. For one typical filing, how long does the e-Filing portal part take, from login to final submit? How long to prepare the documents?
4. Which steps or fields slow you down most? Which do you have to retype again and again?
5. What mistakes or rejections happen most? What was the last filing that got returned, and why (in general terms)?
6. Does a clerk or junior do any of this for you? Who logs in?

## The portal
7. Is anything different from what you expected: extra steps, pop-ups, session time-outs, steps that appear only for some case types?
8. Upload rules you have to follow: file type, size, bookmarks, page limits, naming, one PDF or many, OCR. Anything that trips people up?
9. How do you pay court fee (online mode, e-stamp, calculator)? Is the fee calculated by the portal or by you?
10. Do you use DSC, Aadhaar e-sign, or video oath at any step? Which ones?
11. Which browser, operating system and screen size do you use? Any extensions already installed? Is installing a Chrome extension allowed on your work laptop?
12. Does the portal behave the same on every court, or have you seen differences between courts or states?

## How client details reach you
13. How do clients send you details today (WhatsApp, email, call, in person)? In what form (typed message, photos of documents, PDFs)?
14. What documents do you collect for a typical filing?
15. What would you NOT want filled in automatically, even if the tool is confident?

## Your documents (please share anonymised copies)
16. Your standard templates, with names, addresses and amounts replaced by placeholders: plaint (for money recovery or a commercial suit), vakalatnama, affidavit, list of documents / index, statement of truth, legal notice. One of each is enough.
17. A redacted example of a complete petition bundle (main petition + annexures index) as you upload it.
18. Your own checklist or notes for e-filing, if you have one.

## Trust and rules
19. What would make you trust a tool like this? What would make you stop using it?
20. Do you know of any rule, circular or terms of use of the e-Filing portal that restricts using software to fill forms in your own logged-in session? Please point us to it. We were unable to read the portal's terms page automatically.
21. Anything else we should know before building this?
